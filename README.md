🌍 Our Mission

PawGuard is an educational project designed to raise awareness about animal abuse and promote animal welfare.

Our mission is to inspire people to protect animals through education, responsible ownership, adoption, and community action.

> **Animals cannot always speak for themselves. We can be their voice.**

🔎 Why Does It Happen?

Some factors that contribute to animal abuse include:

• 📚 Lack of education about animal welfare.
• ⚖️ Insufficient enforcement of animal protection laws.
• 🚫 Irresponsible pet ownership.
• 💔 Social indifference toward animal suffering.
• 🏠 Abandonment and lack of access to animal care.

🌱 The Consequences

Animal abuse can lead to:

• Physical suffering and health problems.
• Fear, stress, and behavioral difficulties.
• Increased numbers of abandoned animals.
• Greater pressure on rescue organizations and shelters.
• Threats to wildlife and biodiversity in some cases.

🎯 Project Objectives

Our main objective is to promote animal welfare and encourage responsible actions through an accessible digital platform.

Our specific objectives are:

1. Explain the main forms of animal abuse.
2. Identify factors that contribute to animal mistreatment.
3. Promote practical solutions for protecting animals.
4. Encourage responsible pet ownership and adoption.
5. Use technology to spread awareness and inspire positive change.
